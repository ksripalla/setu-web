Four promises behind every transfer

Setu runs each transfer as one database transaction. These are the four guarantees that gives you, in plain words.

A

All or nothing

The debit, the credit and the receipt are saved together. If anything fails, nothing changes.

C

Rules always hold

No negative balances, no sending to yourself, no amounts with odd decimals. The database enforces it too.

I

No collisions

Two transfers touching the same account wait their turn, so balances are never mixed up.

D

Never lost

Once you see "successful", the transfer is written to disk and survives a crash.

What happens when you press Send

Four steps, all inside one transaction.

  1. Check

    Amount, accounts and your ownership of the sender account are verified.

  2. Lock

    Both accounts are locked in a fixed order, so two opposite transfers can never deadlock.

  3. Move

    The sender is debited and the receiver credited, only if the balance is enough.

  4. Record

    A receipt and two ledger lines are saved, then everything is committed at once.

Questions

What if I click Send twice?

Both clicks carry the same request ID. Setu moves the money once and tells you the second click was already processed.

What if the connection drops mid-transfer?

The transfer is all or nothing. Either it completed and shows in your statement, or no money moved and you can safely try again.

Can someone see my balance?

No. Every balance and statement request is checked against the logged-in user in the database query itself.

Are the amounts exact?

Yes. Amounts are stored as exact decimals with two places, so there are no rounding surprises.
